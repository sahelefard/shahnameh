# Farshchian Miniature Style (Shahnameh Edition)

## Summary

The **farshchian-miniature-style** skill enforces the distinct Persian miniature painting aesthetic of Master Mahmoud Farshchian across all Shahnameh visual and video outputs (`poem2image` and `poem2video`).

## When to Use

- Styling characters, environments, key frames, and animations from the Shahnameh.
- Translating epic battles, royal courts, and tragic moments into authentic modern Persian miniature artworks.

## Steps

1. **Apply Fluid, Expressive Linework (خطوط سیال و پویا):**
   - Use delicate, continuous, lyrical curves to define heroic figures, flowing garments, horse manes, smoke, and swirling clouds.
2. **Enforce Shahnameh Color Palette (پالت رنگی اصیل):**
   - Combine deep ultramarine and indigo, earthy ochres, turquoise, crimson, and luminous gold leaf highlights suited to the dramatic mood of the verse.
3. **Structure Multi-Layered Composition (ترکیب‌بندی و عمق عاطفی):**
   - Intertwine figures, steeds, mythical beasts, and rocky landscapes dynamically around a central emotional focal point, embedding subtle silhouettes within natural textures.
4. **Integrate Traditional Framing (تذهیب و کتیبه‌نویسی):**
   - For static frames (`poem2image`), include ornate Persian tezhib (illumination) borders and traditional nastaliq inscription cartouches when appropriate.

## Gotchas

- Avoid flat, rigid, or generic Western comic/photorealistic rendering; preserve the ethereal gradients and spiritual depth of Master Farshchian's brushwork.
